Who We Are

A two-person practice, built for people who don't have time to onboard a team.

7 Gates Collective is run by a partnership, not a platform. Every engagement is handled personally by one of us — no rotating staff, no ticket queue, no relearning your preferences from scratch.

We built this practice around a simple idea: the people managing the most complex responsibilities deserve support that feels effortless. That means fewer touchpoints, faster judgment calls, and a standard of confidentiality we treat as non-negotiable.
